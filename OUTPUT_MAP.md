# Output Map

This document explains the structure of the `results/` directory.

---

## results/assembly/

Metagenome assemblies generated by MEGAHIT.

---

## results/bins/

Raw genome bins generated by MetaBAT.

---

## results/checkm2/

Quality assessment of bins.

Key file:

* quality_report.tsv (completeness and contamination)

---

## results/filtered_bins/

TSV files listing bins that passed quality thresholds.

---

## results/passed_bins/

Final high-quality MAGs used downstream.

Each sample contains FASTA files:

* bin.X.fa

---

## results/prokka/

Genome annotations for each passed MAG.

Contains:

* .gff
* .faa
* .ffn
* .gbk

---

## results/rgi/

AMR gene predictions from CARD database.

---

## results/antismash/

Biosynthetic gene cluster predictions.

Structure:

sample_antismash/
  bin.X/
    index.html
    region*.gbk
    regions.js

---

## results/strainphlan/

### metaphlan/

Taxonomic profiles and mapping outputs.

### markers/

Marker gene files for strain-level analysis.

### clades/

Detected clades across samples.

Key file:

* print_clades_only.tsv

### final/

Phylogenetic trees and alignments.

Contains:

* RAxML trees (.tre)
* concatenated alignment (.aln)

---

## results/gtdbtk/

Taxonomic classification of MAGs.

(Generated after GTDB-Tk step)

---

## results/pipeline_info/

Pipeline execution metadata:

* execution_report.html → full run report
* trace.tsv → per-process timing/resources
* timeline.html → execution timeline
* dag.html → workflow structure

---

## Interpretation notes

* Only bins passing CheckM2 thresholds are analysed downstream
* antiSMASH runs only on high-quality MAGs
* StrainPhlAn uses MetaPhlAn-derived markers, independent of MAGs
